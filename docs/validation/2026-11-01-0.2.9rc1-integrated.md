## Sustained synthetic durability campaign

The private campaign **PASSed**: 1800 seconds of generation, seed **2601101**,
four simulated PCs and eight worker objects, one PC and one central role per PC,
scheduled sequentially in one Python process. Requested minimum submission
cadence was 1.0 second, verified from omitted `--cadence` and the pinned parser
default; this is invocation metadata, not a result field or guaranteed task rate.
All transport was in-process ASGI/TestClient and `httpx.MockTransport`, with
synthetic responders. No sockets, TLS, inference provider, live service or
instrument was exercised.

GET timeouts occur before dispatch; POST 503s occur before application processing.
Receipt losses occur after the application handler returns; that counter does
not independently verify successful storage or a fresh insert for every loss.
Every approximately 40 seconds the harness closes one client, releases its lock
and recreates the worker over its journal. Server, process, loaded code and call
counter remain alive: this is not OS crash/restart coverage. Generation, faults
and recreations stop at 1800 seconds; drain has a further 90-second deadline.

The harness is private; hash/method/seed identify the run without claiming a
published reproduction program. Review latency is measured after submission
until the loop observes a review, including scheduling/retries but excluding
submission overhead; it is not inference latency. FD/state bytes are sampled
about every 30 seconds and at finalization, not strict ceilings. State-byte
samples precede the final result write. `/proc` describes the current process
image; raw `ru_maxrss` may include an earlier executable image's high-water mark
and must not be treated as campaign RSS or subtracted to estimate growth.

The original oracle checks review completeness, correct PC, aggregate `2*N`
calls/reservations and `3*N` messages, final inbox states, no repeated
`(role, input-message-id)` call, and three extra fault-free ticks. Per-task
reservation mapping is separate below. Product policy is at most two backend
intents **per message** across fallback/retries/restarts. This workload has a
synthetic PC reply and central review per task. Reservations prove accounting,
not provider computation or dispatch timing. Cancellation has separate unit evidence.

The earlier 900-second baseline at `7d24b83` recorded 828 tasks/reviews, 1656
synthetic calls, zero duplicates, 22 object recreations and 985.16 s elapsed.
Its faults continued during drain; the final protocol stops them before drain.
Different protocols/checkpoints do not establish causal speedup, production
capacity, real-inference performance or indefinite idempotency.
